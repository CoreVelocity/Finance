Profit centres
Auto-calculated per region. Revenue = closed-won (HubSpot). Product cost = OxeFit unit cost only (catalog cost ÷ MSRP), from Costing — operational % is no longer charged here (it now sits in the central pool) and commissions are shown separately. Commissions from Commission tab. Sales salaries = sum of the region's reps. Marketing & events are not charged per region — they sit in the central pool and are allocated by revenue share. Reward basis = net operating profit.

      

    


    

      
Central cost centre & company P&L
Defaults are the monthly run-rate from the CoreV P&L (Jan 2025 – Jul 2026 ÷ 19 months); the pool and company P&L scale with the selected period (month · quarter · half · year). Product cost, commissions and sales salaries are excluded here (product cost from Costing, commissions from Commission, salaries from the rep table). Marketing (fixed AED 32,000/mo) and Events (fixed AED 2,500/mo) are included as central lines and allocated across regions by revenue share.
